# Population: Standard Normal
<strong>Parameters/assumptions:</strong>
<p>μ=0, σ=1</p>

## 10.1 What population are you sampling from?
<p> The population that I'm sampling from is called the Standard Normal population. </p>

## 10.2 What did you expect before running the simulation?
<p> I expected before running the simulation that the sample distribution of sample means for the Standard Normal will be "normal" with a sample size of 30. </p>

## 10.3 What sample sizes did you investigate?
<p> The sample sizes that I used for this simulation were n = 10, 20, 30, and 40. </p>

## 10.4 When did the sampling distribution become approximately normal?
<p> The sampling distribution became approximately normal at n = 40.</p>

## 10.5 Why did you choose that value?
<p> I chose that value for several reasons. For one, the kurtosis value at n = 40 was 0.004.</p>
